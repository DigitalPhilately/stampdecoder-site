For modern GB collectors

Scan, identify, and collect modern GB barcoded stamps.

Up to now, you've only collected half the story.

Two barcoded stamps can look identical and still come from different printings, different dates and different sources: a counter sheet, a booklet, a prestige pane, an FDC coil. You can't see that by eye, but the barcode records it.

Stamp Decoder reads that barcode with your phone's camera and tells you, in plain collector terms, exactly which stamp you are holding. Then it keeps a record of what you have.

How it works

Point the camera at the barcode.

The app opens straight to the camera. There is nothing to set up first.

STEP 01

Scan

Hold your phone over the square barcode printed beside the stamp design. It will usually read through clear mounts and sleeves, though glare or curved plastic can get in the way.

STEP 02

Decode

You get the value, colour, issue, print date, origin and any collector notes. The raw barcode data is still there if you want it, but you don't have to read it.

STEP 03

Collect

Save it as Mint or Used, and see which ones you already have and which you're still missing.

FAQ

Common questions

Which stamps does it read?

Any Royal Mail stamp with the square DataMatrix barcode: the 2021 trials, the 2022 rollout, King Charles III definitives, regional issues and Christmas stamps. Older stamps without a barcode can't be scanned.

What do I get free, and what does Pro add?

Scanning and decoding standard barcoded stamps is free, and you can save up to 25 stamps. Pro (99p a month or £9.99 a year) decodes other Royal Mail barcode formats, links warrants and removes the collection limit. See Free and Pro.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning and decoding happen on your phone, so you can use it at a stamp fair with no signal.

Do you collect any data about me?

There's no account and no advertising tracking. Your barcodes, photos and collection never leave your phone. The app sends anonymous usage counts so we can see what's working; the privacy policy has the full detail.

Is this an official Royal Mail product?

No. Stamp Decoder is an independent collector tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Royal Mail Group Ltd. Royal Mail stamp images on this site are used with permission.
