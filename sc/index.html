Welcome, Stamp Collector readers

Thanks for following up the ad. Stamp Decoder reads the barcode on modern Royal Mail stamps and tells you which printing you have: value, colour, print date, origin (counter sheet, booklet, prestige pane, FDC coil) and collector notes. It's free to download, works offline, and runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

On your phone, open the App Store (iPhone or iPad) or Play Store (Samsung and other Android), search for Modern GB Stamp Decoder and install it. Or use the buttons below.
