Thanks for following up the ad. Point Stamp Decoder at the barcode on a stamp on your cover and it tells you at once whether that stamp came from an FDC coil, along with its value, colour, issue date and barcode date. It works the same way for barcoded stamps from counter sheets, booklets and prestige panes. It's free to download, works offline, and runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

On your phone, open the App Store (iPhone or iPad) or Play Store (Samsung and other Android), search for Modern GB Stamp Decoder and install it. Or use the buttons below.
